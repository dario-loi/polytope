## Overall Impression

A strong owned world with weak lookup tools. The places where the reader looks things up (token tables, the Tokens page, search, phone navigation) are the weakest parts of the site. The biggest opportunity is to make finding things as good as the look.

## What's Working

- One owned world, held consistently: one accent with a job, square corners, panels as objects, a full stop as the signature.
- The models teach: the Components "Train model" panel shows button, loader, and status changing together; the Accessibility reduced-motion toggle demonstrates the commitment.
- Example content is honest and labeled; the paper pattern (theorem, numbered equation, sidenote, BibTeX copy) is adoptable as is.

Kit-level, so downstream tables do the same. Fix: overflow-wrap: break-word, or nowrap code inside .ply-table cells. Command: /impeccable harden.

[P1] The Tokens page, the main lookup job, is broken. `.tk td.tk__desc:empty { display: none }` (docs/src/pages/tokens/index.astro:63) applies at every width; 71 of 104 rows have no purpose, so "Used in" slides under the Purpose header and row rules stop short. The lede promises search by name, value, or purpose, yet "spacing" finds 0 and "teal" misses --ply-color-link. Fix: scope :empty to the phone layout, write a purpose for every role token, add aliases, a zero state with a suggestion and a clear button, and sync ?q= to the URL. Command: /impeccable harden.

[P1] No search; "Find a token" is a link styled as an input (docs/src/layouts/Base.astro:30). It goes to /tokens/ without focusing the filter; components, class names, and pages cannot be searched. Fix: Pagefind indexing pages, components, classes, and tokens, with a "/" shortcut; at minimum link to #tk-q and focus the filter. Command: /impeccable shape.
