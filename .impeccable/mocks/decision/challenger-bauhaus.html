introduction

A design system is two things at once: a construction kit you take pieces from, and a design language. Tokens are its points, components join them into lines, and patterns fill those lines into planes: a homepage, a paper page, a demo.

v0.1 is experimental. APIs may change before 1.0.
# add the core to any project
npm install github:darioloi/design-system
